One time only. Saved on this device.

Tap the mic and speak: "roll 1" then the marks.
Mic off

How to use

  1. Setup: enter subjects (with max marks) and students once.
  2. Tap the mic. Pick your language at the top.
  3. Say "roll 5" to select a student, or speak the student's name.
  4. Then say marks: "42" fills the current box and moves ahead. Say several at once: "42 38 45 40".
  5. Say "maths 45" (or "ગણિત 45") to fill one subject directly.
  6. "next" = next student, "clear" = empty the current box, "undo" = undo last entry.
  7. You can also tap any box and type.
  8. Red box = marks above the maximum. Export gives a CSV that opens in Excel.
